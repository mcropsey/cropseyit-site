The lab at a glance

What's on each node

CLASSROOM SERVER · PRE-BUILT

utility

192.168.1.105 · utility.lab.example.com

System

  • Rocky Linux 10.2, 8 vCPU, 3.6 GiB RAM, one 127 GiB disk
  • Don't do lab tasks here. It only serves the other two.

Services

  • dnsmasq (DNS): answers for lab.example.com, forwards everything else to 1.1.1.1 / 8.8.8.8. Config: /etc/dnsmasq.d/lab.conf
  • httpd (repo): BaseOS + AppStream at /rhel10/ (8.7 GB), drop-in at /rhel10.repo
  • httpd (Flatpak): remote at /flatpak/lab.flatpakrepo, GNOME Calculator, unsigned
  • nfs-server: /exports/data rw, /exports/docs ro, /home/guests rw (holds remoteuser1)
  • chronyd (NTP): serves 192.168.1.0/24, local stratum 10

Firewall

  • dns, http, https, nfs, mountd, rpc-bind, ntp, ssh, cockpit
EXAM NODE

server1

192.168.1.103 · server1.lab.example.com

System

  • Rocky Linux 10.2, 8 vCPU, 3.6 GiB RAM
  • SELinux enforcing, firewalld on
  • User mcropsey in wheel (sudo)

Disks

  • sdb 127 GiB: the OS disk (VG rl: root 70G, swap 3.9G, home 50.5G). Never wipe or partition it.
  • sda 5 GiB, empty: Lab 18 LVM (datastore)
  • sdc 5 GiB, empty: Lab 19 swap partition

Installed by the labs

  • httpd, policycoreutils-python-utils, setroubleshoot-server (Lab 3)
  • at (Lab 7), tuned (Lab 21), flatpak (Lab 22), cloud-utils-growpart (Lab 25)
  • Apache on port 82 serving /webdata after Lab 3
EXAM NODE

server2

192.168.1.104 · server2.lab.example.com

System

  • Rocky Linux 10.2, 8 vCPU, 3.6 GiB RAM
  • SELinux enforcing, firewalld on
  • User mcropsey in wheel (sudo)

  • nginx, nmap-ncat (Lab 24): nginx on port 80
  • at (Lab 7), tuned (Lab 21), flatpak (Lab 22)

Prerequisites

  1. Three VMs on one network. server1 (.103), server2 (.104), and utility (.105) on 192.168.1.0/24 with gateway 192.168.1.1. RHEL 10 or Rocky Linux 10 on all three.
  2. utility is up and serving. Check from server1 or server2 before you start: 
    dig +short @192.168.1.105 utility.lab.example.com      # 192.168.1.105
curl -sI http://utility.lab.example.com/rhel10/BaseOS/repodata/repomd.xml | head -1   # 200 OK
showmount -e utility.lab.example.com                    # /exports/data, /exports/docs, /home/guests
chronyc sources                                         # after Lab 9: ^* utility
  3. A sudo user on server1 and server2. The workbook runs everything as a regular user in wheel with sudo.
  4. Two empty extra disks on server1, at least 2 GiB and 1 GiB (here: sda and sdc, 5 GiB each). Confirm with lsblk that they have no partitions before Lab 18.

    Warning: on server1 the OS lives on sdb, not sda. Disk names follow the order the hypervisor attached them, so always pick the disk by size and "no partitions", never by name alone.

  5. Snapshots of server1 and server2 after Lab 1 and again before Lab 3, so you can roll back.
  6. For Lab 25 only: the ability to enlarge server1's OS disk in the hypervisor (Hyper-V refuses while checkpoints exist).
  7. For Lab 17 only: console access to server1 (the VM window), since you work at the GRUB menu.
